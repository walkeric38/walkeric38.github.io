Neural Amp Modeling Guitar Amplifier

Our team is developing a portable, touchscreen-controlled guitar amplifier around Neural Amp Modeler. I’ve helped define the system architecture, select components, and perform testing. My interface work uses C and LVGL on an ESP32-S3 to support amplifier selection, adjustable effects, and signal-chain reordering.

Wi-Fi-Controlled Robot

I built a bidirectional motor driver with MOSFETs and bipolar transistors. An Arduino Nano Every tracks encoder pulses to control how far the robot moves and turns, with wireless control through a web interface and a Raspberry Pi Pico joystick.

Sumo Robot

Our three-person team combined a custom PCB, DC motors, and 3D-printed parts in a competition robot. I developed the Pico-based handheld controller that sent driving commands over infrared.
