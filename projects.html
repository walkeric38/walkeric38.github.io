Custom Arduino Uno

The objective of this project was to design a custom Arduino Uno that preserves the functionality of the commercial board while reducing switching noise.

The layout incorporates a continuous bottom ground plane to provide a low-inductance return path and help minimize ground bounce and switching noise. A custom switching-noise shield enables direct comparison with a commercial Arduino Uno to measure and characterize the effect of the PCB layout on noise performance.
