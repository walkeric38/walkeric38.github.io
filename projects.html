Digital guitar amplifier

Our team is building a portable amplifier around Neural Amp Modeler. I helped map the system and select components. My focus is the 7-inch ESP32-S3 touchscreen. The LVGL prototype lets a player select an amp, adjust effects, and drag them into a different order.

Wi-Fi-controlled robot

I built a bidirectional motor driver with MOSFETs and bipolar transistors. An Arduino Nano Every tracks encoder pulses to control how far the robot moves and turns. I added wireless control through a web interface and a Raspberry Pi Pico joystick.

PCB & robotics
September 2025

Sumo robot

Our three-person team combined a custom PCB, DC motors, and 3D-printed parts in a competition robot. I developed the Pico-based handheld controller that sent driving commands over infrared.
